## Transferable method

1. Choose a **coherent visual foundation** of two colors/roles that work together.
2. Add **one distinct accent role** where you want an audience to notice an action, phrase, or key idea.
3. Let color hierarchy change what the viewer looks at first; don't increase saturation everywhere.
4. Judge color **in context**, not by whether individual swatches look exciting on their own.
5. Restrain the accent so it retains information value.
