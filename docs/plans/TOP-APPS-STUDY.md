## Patterns across the chart

1. **The app does the chore, you approve it.** Muse, ChatGPT, Gemini and
   Claude all moved from "answer" to "act, then ask yes or no." The win is
   one tap instead of five.
2. **Value on the first screen, setup later.** TikTok, Threads and Google show
   something useful before asking anything.
3. **Short, finishable pieces.** PineDrama episodes, TikTok clips, CapCut
   templates. Small enough to start without deciding to.
4. **Living outside the app.** Maps and Gmail put the next step on the Lock
   Screen and Home Screen (Live Activities, widgets), so the app is opened
   less but used more.
5. **It learns you.** TikTok's feed, ChatGPT's memory. Less to set up, more
   that fits.
6. **Smart timing.** Quince's back-in-stock alerts and Gmail's nudges show up
   when the thing is actually relevant, not on a schedule.
7. **Forgiving.** Undo send, edit, drafts. Mistakes cost nothing.
8. **Engagement tricks.** Endless feeds, streak counters, spins, countdown
   deals, money on outcomes (TikTok, Temu, Kalshi, Streaks).

## What fits, and what doesn't

Pattern 8 is out. Brain Navigator's hard lines (DIRECTION.md §8) rule out
streak pressure, endless feeds, slot mechanics and urgency. Pattern 5 already
lives in About you and This-or-that. Pattern 7 already lives in Undo
everywhere. Pattern 3 already lives in "Can't start? Just 5 minutes."

| Pick | Pattern | Idea for us | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Does the chore, you approve | When tonight is over your cap, the Tonight row offers the fix: "Pip can fit it in" opens Pip's plan, which fits the night inside the cap and goes on Your day in one tap. (Setting work aside wouldn't help: it is still due.) | Nothing new. Built in #58. |
| **B** | Short pieces + Anki | Flashcards remember which cards you missed and bring those back first next time. | Nothing new; local only |
| C | Living outside the app | Home Screen widget with the lit card; a Live Activity while the Focus candle burns. | A widget target: new native code and signing. Peter's OK. |
| D | Value first | First run: show the Desk with one sample card before asking for classes. | Design call; touches the welcome |
| E | Smart timing | One quiet reminder when tonight goes over your cap, opt-in, off by default. | Notifications package. Peter's OK. |

A and B come first, since they need no new packages, no signing and no
database.
