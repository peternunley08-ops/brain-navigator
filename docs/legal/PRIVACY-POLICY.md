Brain Navigator is a school planner and wellness companion for high school
students. It is not a medical service, not therapy, and not for emergencies.
It's for people **13 and older** [or 16]; if you're under 13, please don't use it.

## 1. The short version

- Most of what you put in stays **on your phone**.
- Some things are saved to **our database** so they're not lost (listed below). ⚠ Only your phone can read your copy.
- **AI is optional.** Nothing goes to the AI until you agree on the screen that explains it, and you can take that back any time.
- We **don't sell** your data, don't show ads, don't use tracking or analytics, and never use your health information for marketing.

## 2. What we collect, where it lives, and why

| What | Where it's kept | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Classes, assignments, grades, focus sessions, notes, flashcards, colleges, wins, worries, settings | On your phone only | To run the planner and tools |
| Desk entries (what's weighing on you, how heavy), check-ins (mood, stress, sleep), habits, tasks, food and plate logs, sleep-room checks, vents you choose to save, photos you attach to entries | Our database (Supabase, servers in Canada) ⚠ linked to an anonymous ID for your phone only | So they're saved and can show trends |
| Apple Health: sleep, resting heart rate and steps (read); mindful minutes (written) | Read on your phone; sleep hours can be saved with your check-in in our database | To fill in your check-in, only if you allow it in Apple Health |
| Approximate location | Sent to Open-Meteo for the weather; not stored by us | Weather and sunset for the app's sky, only if you allow it |
| Microphone | Speech is turned into text on your phone; audio isn't recorded or uploaded | Speaking instead of typing, only when you tap the mic |
| Photos you pick | Read on your phone (screenshots of schedules or grades); entry photos you attach are saved as above | Reading text from screenshots; your entries |
| Canvas address and access token | On your phone; the token is in the iPhone keychain | To read your Canvas to-do list directly from Canvas |
| Google Calendar | Google's sign-in keeps its tokens in the keychain; events are read on your phone | Showing your calendar, only if you connect it |

We don't collect your contacts, your precise location, advertising identifiers,
or any data for advertising.

## 3. The AI (Ember's "thinking brain" and the helpers)

Ember's replies can be written by **Claude, an AI made by Anthropic**. Ember is a
computer program, not a person, and can't feel emotions. The first time you ask
for an AI reply, the app shows exactly what would be sent and asks for your OK.
Without it, nothing is sent and Ember uses its own built-in questions.

When you've agreed and you ask for a reply, the app sends: what you type in that
chat (with phone numbers, emails and street addresses blanked out on your phone
first), and a short note so the reply fits your day: your first name and grade,
your classes, what's due this week, what's open on your Desk, today's check-in
numbers (which can include sleep from Apple Health), and, only if you turn them
on, About you and things Ember remembers. "Keep my Desk private" leaves the Desk
and check-in out. You can hold any reply to see exactly what was sent.

It goes through our server to Anthropic, which writes the reply. Anthropic's
commercial terms say it doesn't train its models on this data; it keeps it for a
limited time for trust and safety [confirm current period with Anthropic's
terms]. You can turn AI off everywhere, or take back your OK, in
Settings → How AI works here.

## 4. Safety

If something you write sounds like you might be in danger, the app shows crisis
lines right away, on your phone, before and without any AI: call or text **988**
(Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text **HOME to 741741** (Crisis Text Line), and
the Need help now page. Ember's AI is instructed to respond with care, urge you
to reach a real person, and never to give medical advice. In a chat, Ember
reminds you that it's a computer program at the start and at least every three
hours, with a nudge to take a break. These numbers are for the US.

## 5. Who else sees data

Only these service providers, only to run the app, and each bound to protect it
at least as well as this policy: **Supabase** (database), **Anthropic** (AI, only
with your OK), **Open-Meteo** (weather, approximate location), **Apple** (Health,
speech, if you use them), **Google** and **Instructure (Canvas)**, only if you
connect them. We don't sell or share personal data for advertising, and we don't
disclose health data to anyone except as listed here or as the law requires.

## 6. How long we keep it, and deleting it

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or the app. ⚠ Data in our database
is kept while you use the app and deleted [12] months after your phone last
syncs, or within [30] days of your request. Settings → Start fresh clears your
phone ⚠ and our copy. To delete everything, or to ask what we hold, email
[email]; we answer within [30/45] days. You can also withdraw any consent
(AI, Health, location, microphone) at any time in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Mood, stress, sleep, worries, vents and Health data are consumer health data. We

section 5 (the AI only with your separate OK), never sell them, and never use
them for ads or geofencing. Your rights: to know, access, delete and withdraw

## 8. Teens and children

The app is meant for students 13 and older [or 16]. We don't knowingly collect
data from children under 13; if we learn we have, we delete it. Parents can
contact [email]. ⚠ Where state law requires, the app reads the App Store's age
category and respects parental consent.

## 9. Security

Tokens are kept in the iPhone keychain; connections use HTTPS; ⚠ each phone can
read only its own data. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and
regulators as the law requires.

We'll show a notice in the app before any change that affects how your data is
used, and ask again where the law requires consent.
