# What this app is

Brain Navigator is a small, quiet app for one high school student. It keeps school, and the rest of life around it, from piling up.

It lives in a candlelit night room. Ember, a small flame, keeps you company. Ember says things in plain words, notices what is going on, and never nags. There are no streaks, no guilt and no scoreboard. If a day goes badly, the app is the same warm room the next morning.

## What it holds

- **The Desk** is the front page: what is next, one thing to start with, countdowns, pinned notes.
- **School** keeps classes, assignments with due dates, grades and focus sessions.
- **Focus** is a place to sit down and work for a while.
- **Steady** is for the other part of being a student: sleep, winding down, worries, small wins.
- **The drawer** holds everything else: notes, colleges, study helpers and their games, plans, About you.

## What it does for you

It tells you early when a night holds more homework than you set as your limit. It notices when you are short on sleep. It lets you put a worry in the worry box and come back to it later. It keeps your small wins so you can look back at them.

## How it treats you

- Calm, plain, sentence-case words. No pressure.
- Everything can be skipped and changed later, including the first-run welcome.
- Delete anything and you get a few seconds to undo it.
- Your schoolwork stays on your phone. Scanning a screenshot of your classes is read on the phone and nothing is uploaded. Nothing new goes to a server.
- AI chat is optional, limited, and can be turned off.

## The first run

Ember says hello, asks what to call you and your grade, helps you add your classes (by scan or by typing), introduces the helper crew, asks how much a school night should hold, and ends with five This-or-that cards so Ember can get to know you. Every step can be skipped.
